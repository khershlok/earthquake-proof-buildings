Earthquake-Proof Buildings

The main rule of aseismic design: the building should bend and absorb the shaking instead of resisting it rigidly. Each panel shows the same earthquake hitting two buildings: without the technique on the left and with it on the right. Turn the strength down to see where each one starts to fail.

Simple physics models (spring-and-damper sways), exaggerated so you can see the effect. Not to scale.
